Anyone who works in destination management knows this tension: a city can produce eight, maybe ten excellent guided tours. But visitors arrive with thousands of different wishes. A niche interest, ninety spare minutes, a rainy afternoon with two children. Most of those wishes go unserved, not because the destination lacks substance, but because nobody can produce a tour for every one of them.

With CityBot's AI tours, nobody has to. The visitor states the wish, and the tour builds itself.

---

## What your guest experiences

Picture a visitor stepping out of the main station in Zürich. She's curious about the city's industrial past, has two hours, and knows nobody. She opens CityBot, types "industry and money: how this city got rich", picks five stops, and taps go.

A few minutes later, she's walking through Zürich's old lanes and along the Limmat, on a tour telling her the story of foundries, trading houses, and quiet fortunes:

- **Starting at her feet.** No detour to a tourist office, no fixed meeting point. The route begins exactly where she stands.
- **Walking like a local.** Stops come in a sensible order, at comfortable walking distances. She's never sent zigzagging across town.
- **Listening to the city's secrets.** At every spot and on the way between, a narrated audio guide explains why this place matters: the people, the dates, the anecdotes. All at her pace, with no group and no schedule.

That is what we mean by a local in your pocket who knows you: it listens first, then builds a tour tailored to exactly one person, on request.

## Why you can let the AI speak for your destination

A fair concern, and the most common one we hear: what if the AI makes things up? It doesn't get the chance. Every stop on an AI tour is a real, map-verified place, and every story is grounded in checked, cited sources, reviewed for quality before the visitor ever hears it. Your destination gets a storyteller, not a fabulist.

---

## What this means for your destination

Each part of that visitor experience translates directly into destination value:

- **A personal tour on request** means an effectively infinite tour catalogue at zero production cost. Your team curates the signature tours; the AI covers the long tail.
- **Tours that start anywhere** spread visitor flows beyond the top three sights. Lesser-known quarters and hidden gems get walked past, noticed, visited.
- **Verified places and fact-checked stories** mean your destination is presented credibly. There is no reputation risk from an AI inventing attractions in your name.
- **Every wish typed in is a data point.** For the first time, you see what visitors actually want from your destination: themes requested, times of day, group contexts. That's demand research no survey can match.

---

## Curated meets personal

To be clear: AI tours don't replace expert-curated content; they multiply it. Take Zug as an example: its Cherry Tour and Crypto Tour are the destination's signature pieces, crafted by people who know the city best. The same holds for Zürich and every other destination. The AI tours cover everything in between: the individual wish, the odd timeframe, the specific mood of one specific Tuesday. The result is a destination that has an answer for every visitor, not just the average one.
